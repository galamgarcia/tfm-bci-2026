01 / THE GAME

Escape the system

Bit is a tiny digital entity moving through a world of stable geometry and mental possibilities. Each level introduces another way for the player to affect the simulation until every mechanic has to work together.

01

Physical world

Black geometry is stable, predictable and real. It gives the player safe places to observe, prepare and act.

03

04 / MASTER'S THESIS

BCI interaction applied to videogames

BIT was developed as the playable prototype of a Master's Thesis in Multimedia Engineering at the University of Alicante. The project explores how consumer EEG signals and head-based interaction can be transformed into understandable videogame mechanics.

The prototype uses BrainLink Pro for EEG-derived cognitive metrics and a mobile device for head tracking.
